

\chapter[ROHSA parameters]{ROHSA parameters}
\label{app:rohsa}

Table~\ref{tab:rohsa_common_parameters} gives the settings used for the final ROHSA decompositions described in Section~\ref{sec:ROHSA}. The amplitude and global dispersion penalties are zero, while the centroid and local dispersion penalties are both one. Each galaxy is fitted with two or three Gaussian components per spectrum. These Gaussians describe the shape of the emission profile; their number does not represent the number of thermal phases. The minimum fitted dispersion is $\sigma_{\min}=1.274\,\Delta v$, where $\Delta v$ is the velocity-channel spacing. This corresponds to three channels across the Gaussian FWHM and prevents the fit from assigning widths narrower than this adopted sampling limit. The bound therefore differs between galaxies because their channel spacings differ.
